# Voiceover draft

A strong social video starts with a clear reason to watch. This prompt helps you turn your project into a focused idea, a short script, and a production plan that fits the footage and tools you already have. Bring the audience, format, budget, and anything you need protected. It asks what’s missing, then shapes the next steps around your setup. Use it to get from “we should make a video” to a plan you can actually finish.
