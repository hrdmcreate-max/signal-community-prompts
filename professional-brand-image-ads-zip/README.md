# Image Ad Campaign Planning and Testing Kit

**What it helps with:** Plan a small campaign of image-ad concepts, review criteria, and a simple way to compare results.

**Where to use it:** Paste the starter prompt into an AI chat. Use image tools or analytics only when available and authorized.

**What to bring:** Bring your project, audience, campaign goal, approved offer, channels, timeframe, assets, measurement access, budget, and risk limits.

The starter prompt asks about your project and desired outcome, checks for critical gaps, then adapts its help to your answers. It does not assume every reader has the same tools or account access. Any paid service, account connection, or public action remains subject to your choice and authorization.

Full workflow: see the [complete workflow](complete-workflow.md) for the testing playbook.
