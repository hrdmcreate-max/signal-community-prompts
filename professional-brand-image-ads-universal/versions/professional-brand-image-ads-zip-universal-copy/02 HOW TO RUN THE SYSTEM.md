Replace scheduler/image-tool steps with the services you actually use and verify their current official documentation, account access, plan limits, and costs. This document is guidance, not permission to connect, spend, schedule, or publish. For a tool-flexible workflow, start with the package-level `advanced-workflow.md`.

# How to run the universal system

This guide turns the master prompt into a practical first-month workflow. Use current tools and platform requirements; menu labels and subscription rules may change.

## What you need

A capable agent with image generation, file access, live research, and authorized account/browser tools; your brand identity; truthful offerings/conditions; exact selected destinations; and enough capacity for the proposed month.

If the chat cannot operate accounts, it can still create the prompts, images, captions, calendar, and upload packet. It must not claim that it connected or scheduled anything.

## Step 1 — provide the brand packet

Attach the real logo and approved variants, colors/fonts or style examples, official website, service/product facts, CTA, owned/licensed assets, and completed intake.

The ten intake groups cover outcome, audience, identity, offers, destinations, scheduler tier, calendar, links, resources/budget, and review/automation. The agent should prefill existing information rather than ask you to repeat it.

Choose the execution mode explicitly:

- **Draft-only:** all work stays prepared for review.
- **Schedule after sample approval:** once you approve the direction and agreed plan, the agent completes ordinary scheduling within that scope.
- **Review every item:** each exact final image/caption requires your review before scheduling.

Connection permission and publishing permission are separate. Paid ads, subscription purchases, profile changes, and perpetual content generation need their own specific scope.

## Step 2 — discover public social candidates

Start with supplied account URLs and links on the brand’s official website. Compare names, handles, domains, descriptions, and cross-links. Use focused public search when needed.

A same-name profile may belong to someone else. The agent records the source and confidence, then verifies ownership/admin access through the appropriate signed-in account or permissioned tool. Unverified candidates remain excluded.

“Every account” means every verified account for the selected brand within your specified allowlist and supported route. It does not mean every account in the browser, every account the owner manages, or every network in a scheduler’s marketing list.

## Step 3 — verify scheduler identity before connecting

Inspect the actual signed-in identity, workspace/brand, exact destination, and account type. Avoid unrelated clients and personal/creator profiles.

Prefer appropriate authorized MCP/API/connector tools. Resolve their capabilities just in time. An approximate tool search may return a different vendor; do not use that result as a substitute scheduler.

Use the official browser connection flow when an applicable connector is unavailable. Complete credentials and security steps in the service; never paste secrets into chat.

At an OAuth/access screen, confirm what app, account, and scope are actually shown and follow current action-time rules. Broad permissions can include features outside your job, such as advertising access. Granting those permissions does not authorize launching ads.

After approval, verify the selected account and saved connection. Do not stop at the callback URL or a closing popup.

## Step 4 — check tier, formats, and capacity

Read the live plan page, remaining usage, queued content, trial expiry, and current first-party help.

Metricool’s documentation checked October 7, 2026 lists Free as one brand/up to 20 posts monthly, with LinkedIn/X excluded; API access and MCP access have different plan requirements. Recheck live entitlement for every new run rather than treating this snapshot as permanent. [Metricool plan/access guide](https://help.metricool.com/plans-add-ons-and-api-access-explained-xux1u)

For LinkedIn, distinguish member profile and organization Page. Metricool’s documented connection flow selects one or the other within a brand; a company Page needs the documented admin role. Verify current rules and the actual selected identity. [Metricool LinkedIn connection guide](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-connect-linkedin-to-metricool-jq80g)

Count deliveries, not just images. If you have 30 days, two daily slots, and two platforms, the proposal may need 120 destination deliveries. Existing usage and trial expiry matter.

Resolve the capacity gap before bulk production. Use the already-approved native fallback, reduce cadence with agreement, or deliver the unscheduled remainder as drafts. No silent upgrade.

Account support does not imply every post format is supported. Do not invent a still-image posting route on a video-only flow. Do not turn images into videos without the appropriate production scope.

## Step 5 — build the content matrix

Create asset/content IDs and a calendar with date, local time, named zone, platform/account, pillar, message, scene, subject, layout, ratio, caption/link, offer expiry, and status.

Keep identity/voice/claims fixed. Vary people, natural actions, business environments, framing, light, neutral/accent balance, and suitable ratios.

Use one message and one action per image. Avoid a month of near-identical faces or repeated scenes. Realistic people are a choice for suitable brands, not a requirement for every product, food, or illustration campaign.

## Step 6 — approve a representative sample

The agent enhances a concrete concept into the full structured prompt, supplies readable reference images, and generates one complete ad directly.

Review the actual image, not only the prompt:

- Is the brand recognizable?
- Is the person/product believable?
- Is the message readable immediately?
- Are every word, logo, price/date, URL, and footer correct?
- Is the footer smaller and centered, yet readable on a phone?
- Does the caption match the asset?
- Does the design stay original?

Approve the exact direction/revision. If it fails, identify the concrete defect and repair it before scaling.

## Step 7 — produce the batch with review

Use a full prompt per unique asset and a subject/style ledger. Save originals, rejects, and numbered finals separately.

Check full size and phone-feed size. OCR may help with transcription, but the agent must inspect logos, faces, anatomy, composition, and terms visually.

A narrow edit preserves successful work; recheck all copy afterward. If a model repeatedly fails a mandatory logo/text constraint, flag the asset and stop at the agreed retry cap. Do not mark it “passed” to complete a count.

## Step 8 — captions and actual link behavior

Write captions suited to the network’s audience. Include accurate context, one CTA, material conditions, relevant hashtags, and the verified destination.

The URL printed in an image is not an interactive button. Caption-link behavior differs by platform. Use the actual supported link route, and do not silently alter a bio or profile.

Use helpful alt text where supported. Treat generated people as illustrative imagery, never proof of customer endorsement. Real portfolio inserts require actual authorized screenshots.

## Step 9 — upload and schedule

Use supported direct uploads, applicable tools, or the live import route.

Metricool’s CSV guide instructs using its current template, UTF-8 text, matching date/time format, explicit network selections, and direct public media URLs; it recommends imports of up to 50 rows. This kit’s planning CSV is not that template, and import batching does not change plan quota. [Metricool CSV guide](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-schedule-posts-in-batch-with-a-csv-file-in-metricool-3zwxj)

If public media hosting is not approved, prefer supported local/direct upload. A local filesystem path is not a public URL. Do not expose private files to make bulk import work.

Verify one saved scheduled item first, then continue the agreed batch. For every item check selected networks, destination, final image/crop, full caption, date/time, zone, and publication mode. Notification/reminder publishing is not automatic delivery.

Reload the queue and reconcile saved rows after the batch. Keep incomplete destinations separate. A timeout may have completed the action; check before retrying.

## Step 10 — verify the result

Return final assets, prompts, captions, calendar, account/capacity audit, QA, costs, publication ledger, and native evidence.

A good report states:

“X images passed and were approved. Y destination deliveries are verified scheduled. Z remain drafts/blocked because [specific cause]. These are the exact accounts, saved times, and proof locations.”

It never calls future schedules published, a callback connected, an import processed public, or a local ledger native proof.

## Step 11 — recurring automation, if requested

A month of auto-publish schedules is finite future delivery. Recurring content creation/monitoring requires a saved job on an available automation system.

Specify accounts, dates/end date, action scope, approval rule, production allowance, subscription/ad caps, deduplication, failure behavior, and notifications. Verify the real job ID/status/next run.

If no recurring tool exists, provide the runnable prompt/calendar and label it uninstalled. A text file titled “automation” is not an active service.

## Adding a new platform later

Supply the exact new account and requested action. Reuse approved assets when the format fits. Recheck account type, permission, quota, link behavior, crop, and live history. Create new per-destination records. Do not inherit old publishing authority or duplicate the entire old calendar without checking dates and conflicts.

## Useful start messages

**Images only**

~~~text
Use the attached universal master and brand packet. Create one sample,
then the agreed image batch after my style approval. This run is
image/caption production only. Use the stated production allowance.
~~~

**First month with Metricool**

~~~text
Use the attached universal master and completed intake. Discover the
official business social accounts and verify the selected destinations.
Connect those accounts through the official flow, subject to required
access/terms confirmations. After I approve the sample and feasible
calendar, schedule the agreed organic month to those exact accounts.
Preserve existing content. No paid ads or upgrades.
~~~

**Another destination, unchanged creative**

~~~text
Audit this exact new account, format, queue, and plan. Reuse the approved
assets and captions unchanged where supported. Flag any required crop,
link, or format change before altering them. Prepare the proposed
schedule first; submit only in the review mode I specify.
~~~
