# Voiceover draft

This starter kit helps you carry an ad idea through the useful parts: concepts, exact copy, image prompts, captions, and a review checklist. It first learns your brand, audience, channels, and what you can truthfully say. Then it adapts to your tools and budget, leaving unknowns open instead of guessing. Use it when you want a tidy set of drafts ready for your own review.
