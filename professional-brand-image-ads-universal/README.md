# Professional Brand Image Ads — Universal Kit

**What it helps with:** Build a coherent set of professional image ads with clear copy, visual direction, and quality checks.

**Where to use it:** Paste the starter prompt into an AI chat. Text planning works in chat; image creation needs an image tool, and account scheduling needs authorized account tools.

**What to bring:** Bring your brand or project, audience, goal, approved claims and assets, channels, available tools, budget, review preference, and protected details.

The starter prompt asks about your project and desired outcome, checks for critical gaps, then adapts its help to your answers. It does not assume every reader has the same tools or account access. Any paid service, account connection, or public action remains subject to your choice and authorization.

The included detailed kit has one Metricool-oriented version with official Metricool references. If you use a different scheduler, adapt that section to its current documentation; no account is connected by these files. The short starter and main advanced workflow are tool-flexible. The archive variants are grouped under `versions/`.
