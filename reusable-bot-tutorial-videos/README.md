# Reusable Bot and Software Tutorial Videos

**What it helps with:** Explain a bot or software feature in a short, helpful tutorial that viewers can follow.

**Where to use it:** Use the starter prompt in an AI chat for the script and production plan. Recording and editing require your chosen screen-capture or video tools.

**What to bring:** Bring the product or feature, viewer skill level, one task to teach, safe demo account/data, runtime, tools, budget, and any private information to avoid.

The starter prompt asks about your project and desired outcome, checks for critical gaps, then adapts its help to your answers. It does not assume every reader has the same tools or account access. Any paid service, account connection, or public action remains subject to your choice and authorization.

Full workflow: see the [complete workflow](complete-workflow.md) for the tutorial planning, recording, and review steps.
