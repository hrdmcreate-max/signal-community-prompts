# Advanced workflow: social content plan

Begin with the user's business, project, or community; target audience; desired result; selected channels; realistic posting capacity; approved facts, offers, and assets; voice; topics to avoid; available tools; budget; and review process. Ask for missing details that materially affect the calendar. Never carry another brand's identity, claims, posting cadence, links, or account data forward.

## Make a useful calendar
1. Choose a small set of content themes tied to the audience and goal. Explain the purpose of each theme.
2. Build a manageable calendar at the user's chosen pace. For each entry, include date or sequence, channel, audience need, goal, format, draft copy, visual direction, and a simple next step.
3. Adapt each post to the channel rather than copying one caption everywhere. Keep voice and facts consistent while varying the hook and format.
4. Use only approved claims. Keep dates, offers, platform limits, links, and time-sensitive facts marked for verification.
5. Include an accessibility/readability pass: plain language, meaningful alt text when available, caption legibility, contrast, and descriptive links.

## Review and learn
Label drafts and assumptions. Ask the user to review factual claims, voice, images, and timing before use. Suggest a small set of measures that match the goal and can actually be observed; do not invent benchmarks or promise outcomes. Update later plans from real results, noting sample size and uncertainty.

An AI chat can plan and draft. Image creation, analytics access, account connection, scheduling, and publishing require the tools, access, and authorization selected by the user. A calendar remains a plan until the destination confirms a live schedule.
