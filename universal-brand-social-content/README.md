# A Month of Brand Social Content

**What it helps with:** Plan a month of social content with distinct ideas, platform-aware copy, and a reviewable schedule.

**Where to use it:** Paste the starter prompt into an AI chat. Image creation or scheduling requires the tools and account access you choose.

**What to bring:** Bring your business or project, audience, goals, channels, posting pace, approved facts/assets, tools, budget, and approval workflow.

The starter prompt asks about your project and desired outcome, checks for critical gaps, then adapts its help to your answers. It does not assume every reader has the same tools or account access. Any paid service, account connection, or public action remains subject to your choice and authorization.

Full workflow: see the [complete workflow](complete-workflow.md), which gathers the complete source prompt, image guide, and brief.
